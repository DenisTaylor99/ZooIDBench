# Image provenance, attribution, and licenses

Original photographs were sourced from **iNaturalist** observations under **individual photographer licenses**, documented in `data/ORIGINAL_SPECIMEN_REGISTRY.md` (source observation URL, credited photographer, photo identifier, CC license, attribution wording, and recorded modifications).

At least some source images are marked **CC BY-NC 4.0**, **CC BY 4.0**, **CC BY-SA 4.0** or **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. The original registry includes a blanket `Publication permitted? yes` field; this should **not be interpreted as legal permission for every type of publication, commercial reuse, model training, rehosting, or relicensing**. In particular, NC restrictions matter for commercial use; SA restrictions matter when distributing adaptations; attribution and preservation of notices apply according to the relevant license.

This release deliberately **does not include the image files**. It includes the registry as a source/attribution ledger, not a blanket redistribution license.
