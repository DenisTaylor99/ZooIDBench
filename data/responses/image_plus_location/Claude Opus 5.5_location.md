### img_001.png (Simalia amethistina)

Identification: _Simalia amethistina_  
Subspecies status: No recognized subspecies  
Confidence:

- Genus: High
- Species: Medium-High
- Subspecies: N/A

Visual evidence: The head carries large, symmetrical crown shields (enlarged prefrontals and frontal region) rather than the small fragmented scales of _Morelia_, together with labial heat pits and a long, slender, arboreal build. The tan ground colour with irregular dark zigzag crossbands, the darkening posterior body, and the faint bluish iridescence are typical of the amethystine python. Milne Bay Province lies within the range of _S. amethistina_ sensu stricto; the main caveat is that the New Guinea amethistine complex is taxonomically unsettled, and the former subspecies _kinghorni_ is now treated as a separate Australian species.
